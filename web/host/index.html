Soundboard

Upload short clips - laughs, applause, transition stings - then fire them one-click from the Soundboard button in a session's host controls. Each clip can be played over everyone, or with everyone muted for its length. They're mixed into the recording and the live stream, and saved as a separate track. Up to 20 clips. MP3, WAV, OGG, AAC, M4A or WebM, up to 5 MB each.

Intro videos

Upload short intro or transition videos, then fire one from the Soundboard button in a session's host controls. It takes over every screen fullscreen, mutes everyone while it plays, and returns to the grid when it ends - baked into the recording and the live stream. Up to 5 videos. MP4 or WebM, up to 80 MB each; anything above 720p (or in a heavy codec) is converted at upload so it plays smoothly on every guest's machine. Keep them short (a few seconds).
